Travel distance calculator

Type where each journey started and ended, then pick from the suggestions. One-way distances only. For a return trip, don't double it.

Start typing and choose a suggestion for the most accurate result. You don't need your exact home address: your suburb or nearest landmark is accurate enough.

How is this calculated?

Air and ferry use straight-line distance. Vehicle uses the driving route. Rail is estimated from the driving route, because no free rail routing exists. Results are estimates for carbon footprint reporting.

Privacy: this tool doesn't store or collect anything. As you type, the text is sent to Photon (a search service built on OpenStreetMap data) to show suggestions, and to OpenStreetMap and OSRM to find the distance.
